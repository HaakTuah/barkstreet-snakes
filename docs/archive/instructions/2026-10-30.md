In the design chat (30 October 2026), feedback from playtesting:

> - Wildcard's hair looks wrong now. The very first or second hairstyle it had in the game is better than what it has
>   right now.
> - Splashing looks like eating. It should leave squashed beads behind.

>   pretty big trade-off for getting to spit on someone.

>   up in front of it. Also, it doesn't quite know what to do when a snake hits it dead on, so that must be accounted

> - I see snakes jumping onto small islands where they have no chance to survive for very long.
> - Do the snakes have a bit too good a reaction time? This is just a question; they seem to play a bit too well in
>   that regard.
> - We discussed making the regular leap able to close any gap, so it could carry you across to any island. I still see
>   two islands form at opposite ends with the snakes trapped on each, so the one on the bigger one wins.
