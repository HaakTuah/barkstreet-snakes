# The slop bar

What "nothing reads machine-made" means for everything a player reads, and how it is held (archive, 2 November 2026). Reviews of text pass at a 10 only when
nothing reads machine-made, every voice is its own and no formula repeats (`core/modules/reviews.md`, "Only a 10
passes").

- **Scope:** every voice's chatter, impressions, moments and talk (`chatter/`), each rival's blurb, the lessons, the
  Field guide, banners, cards, menus, the news lines and the README's player text.
- **The check:** a fresh-eyes reviewer (a helper given only the text and this file, Opus at high effort) reads each
  voice or screen whole and lists its worst lines, each with the tell: one sentence shape filled in again and again,
  filler and hedging, clichés, every line exclaimed, two voices that sound alike, the same joke twice, lines too long to
  read in play.
- **The fix:** rewritten in each character's own voice, keeping every trademark and each line count's purpose; the PR
  shows ten lines before and after, and the owner's read of them is the final word. One PR per voice group or screen,
  in the polish lane.
- **The guard:** the chatter suite fails a voice with more than four lines of one shape (the same opening words and
  punctuation), so the formula cannot creep back.
