## The snake as a character

Your snake is the protagonist, so it gets the most life:
- **Eyes that glance at the nearest food:** white sclera with ink pupils that ease toward the target.
- **A blink** every 3–6 s, at random.
- **A forked tongue flick** every few seconds, more often when food is close.
- **A body that tapers to the tail**, with **smooth rounded curves at corners**. The spine follows the real cells, so the body never cuts a corner. **It's a caterpillar: one round segment per cell**, each joined to the next by a narrower band and lit on its upper left, so you can count a snake's length at a glance. (For a while bodies were smooth tubes; they looked less alive and hid how long a snake was, so the segments came back. The shadow and outline follow the same pinched shape.)
- **A head that turns smoothly and holds its line:** it swings round over a moment when the snake turns, and when it turns again and again (a staircase, a wiggle), it holds a steady line between the two ways instead of whipping round every tick (which flung Joker's party blower, and every other tongue, from side to side). Since round four that steady line is the rivals' only: yours turns in about 60 ms (`FACE_EASE_MS`), even in swift play, as soon as your head is drawn heading the new way.
- **A bite:** the mouth opens when food is right ahead, the head squashes at the bite and chews twice, and a gulp bulge travels down the body, one segment per tick.
- **A bright cream outline with a soft halo**, so you find yourself at a glance without relying on green. Friends online get a thinner, plainer outline: people still read as people, but only you glow.

The rivals keep their faces and patterns, and share the blink, the bite and the gulp. **Only you (and friends online) have a real forked tongue.** Every few seconds each rival flicks out something that fits it instead, on the same out-and-back rhythm, each at its own pace, so the gag reads as the snake's tongue: Wildcard slides out a dollar bill, Joker toots a party horn that unrolls from its curl, its fringed tip fluttering and a puff of confetti at full stretch, Desperado pokes out a silver six-shooter (bang: a flash and a puff of smoke), Sniper's laser sight blinks on from its scope, Sovereign rolls out the red carpet for itself, Colonist draws out a brass spyglass to look for land, Mason unrolls a papyrus scroll (Latin, naturally), Metagamer (who now wears red-and-cyan 3D glasses, in a card frame outlined so it reads on its cyan head) slides out a keyboard to type up its notes on you, keys lighting up as it types, Vulture chews on a bone, and Star flicks out a tiny spinning star on a ribbon of rainbow, a glint at full stretch (*since round six A* it twirls its mustache instead: see "Star's mustache"). Gobbler licks its lips with a fat pink tongue, often (more near food); Creeper's tongue is the snakiest, long and wriggling; Drab's is short and limp and can't be bothered; Ghost breathes out a wisp of ectoplasm. Capricious flicks its mood's, and Copycat copies its model's (with no one to copy, it bleps like a cat). Everything that comes out is drawn with a dark outline or a bright colour so it still reads at phone size, and it's drawing only: none of it touches the game. **Every snake looks at something**, and what it looks at says who it is (drawing only: it never changes how anyone plays). Gobbler eyes the nearest food, its eyes growing as it gets close; Star the nearest golden power (a golden beetle, leaf or mushroom), the thing it craves. Sniper swings its scope onto its target, Sovereign and Colonist watch intruders (Colonist otherwise eyes its next flag), Vulture watches the snake it expects to die, Copycat its model, Creeper the tail it rides, and Ghost, Metagamer, Desperado and Joker the nearest head. Drab lazily eyes the nearest food under heavy lids, and Mason keeps an eye on whoever is near his wall. **Mason is Caesar in marble:** a warm white body with grey veins wandering across every segment (now and then a gold one), so with his laurel wreath his head reads as a marble bust. (He used to be grey stone bricks: a builder, but not a Caesar.) With nothing of its own to watch, any snake watches the nearest head, so no face ever just stares ahead. Wildcard has an orange tan under a puffy golden combover swept across and forward to a curl over one eye, a white collar and a long red tie that bends with its rainbow body; it squints, pouts, and lets its eyes wander wherever they like. Most have small white eyes with a catchlight, closed eyes that curve like a cartoon's when they blink, and a little mouth; Ghost, Creeper and Copycat keep their special eyes, which still turn, and Vulture's beak gapes instead. Vulture glares from under slanting brows over a big hooked beak, pale horn with a dark tip, outlined so it still reads at phone size; Joker wears a harlequin diamond round each eye, in its hat's purple and orange. Sniper never blinks on the job; knocked out, its squint becomes a cross and the scope slumps off to the side, lens cracked. **Capricious starts every round undecided** (its own pink self, one eye big, one small, a brow cocked, playing it safe) for 3.5–5.5 s before its first mood, and dithers like that for 1.2–2 s between moods. Before, it picked a mood on its very first move, so you never saw its own face. Gobbler wolfs its food down: a gulp twice the size, and hamster cheeks either side of its mouth that puff up with every bite and slowly go down. Capricious gets a permanent pink head ring, so you can always find it whatever mood it's in. The ring breathes, and pale dashes run round it. **Wildcard wears the Stars and Stripes** behind his tan, white collar and red tie: two segments of navy with white stars, then red and white stripes, with the white collar segment kept as it is (the rainbow went to Mute, and on to its moods). **Mute** is a mood-ring mime: white greasepaint, a black beret, a painted tear and small dark lips, its body (and the blush on its cheeks) the colour of how it feels, its brows and mouth acting it out (see Looks and lines); its painted lips part and a second, inner jaw shoots out, snapping (it used to be a white glove, waving); it never speaks, so its bubbles are mimed (*pulls an invisible rope*, *draws a box in the air around you*), and it plays a boxer-in: it picks a target and squeezes its room (wins 28% of its rounds in the simulator, mid-pack). **Capricious does impressions:** in a mood it speaks that rival's own lines (its Mason quotes Latin, its Ghost says "…"), and taking up a mood it does a turn and can't help asking how it went ("Headshot! …Is that how Sniper says it?").
